# Project change tracking

For meaningful user-visible changes, update CHANGELOG.md with the date in America/Chicago, the feedback or requirement, the resulting behavior, and validation actually performed. Keep entries concise and newest first. Preserve existing history and distinguish local verification, code pushes, and confirmed deployments. Do not claim tests or deployment succeeded without evidence.

Keep README.md and the About tab as short entry points linking to the log rather than duplicating the full history.
